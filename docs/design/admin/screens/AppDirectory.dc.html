App directory

Residents see this in the Taytay app: the offices they can find, the Tulong pages that tell them about a service, and the numbers they can call. A change here reaches every resident the next time the app loads.

Offices

The offices residents find in the app’s directory. An office named by a staff account, a Balita article, a Tulong page or a conversation cannot be deleted until those are moved.

Tulong pages

What the app tells residents about a service — what it is, who it is for, what to bring and whom to call. A Tulong page is information only: residents read it, and cannot apply through it. It is not one of the office’s assistance programmes.
