# UAT dry run, 9 November 2026

Before the office runs the UAT round, the developer clicked through the newest cases of
[uat-test-scripts.md](uat-test-scripts.md) (version 1.7), and a sample of older ones, on a local
copy of the platform. The aim was to find cases whose steps or expected results do not match the
product, and product defects, so that the office's round does not stall on them.

**How it was run.** The staff website (built for a local API) and the API (the backend's in-memory
development server) ran on the developer's computer, with the test accounts of the README (ADMIN,
HEAD, SW1, SW2, INTAKE, RELEASE, BLINK, AUDITOR) and fictional Halimbawa test data, all created
through the API. Staging was not touched. A script drove a browser through each case's steps and
compared the words on the page with the expected result. The residents' app was not used: where a
case needs a resident to act (register for an event, give up a place), the same request was sent to
the API on the resident's behalf, and the app's own screens were not checked.

**What the results mean.**

- **Pass**: every step run on the staff website matched the expected result. "Website steps only"
  means the case also has steps in the residents' app, which were not run.
- **Fail**: a step did not match. The note says which, and whether the case text or the product is
  wrong. Product defects are listed under the table.
- **Not run**: the case needs the residents' app or email throughout.

E2 (EVT-08, EVT-09) was shortened: it started 6 minutes after it was created and ran 5 minutes,
instead of 45 minutes and an hour. Figures that depend on the test data (for example REL-05's money
total) differ from staging's and were not compared.

**Totals: 91 cases touched. 64 Pass, 6 Fail, 21 Not run.** Three cases had their text corrected
(ACC-04, RPT-08, EVT-17) in version 1.8 of the scripts.

## Product defects

None of these was changed in the code during the dry run. The severity follows the README's scale.
All twelve were fixed the same day (console `3813c27`, backend `dc1d571`; the rule for refusals is
the console's `DL-196`), and reach staging only when it is redeployed. Each "Fixed" note says what
changed. Five cases follow the new wording in version 1.9 of the scripts: ROS-05, WRK-06, EVT-07,
GOV-01 and NFR-06.

1. **Critical. A barangay link or a release officer cannot open a resident's record.** The record
   page also asks the API for the person's home visits; the API refuses those two roles, and the
   website then leaves the page for "You do not have access to that section", although the page
   is meant to leave the visits panel empty. Seen in ROS-05 and NFR-06 (both Must). Files:
   `src/app/features/residents/resident-detail-page.ts` (the visits read, about line 167) and
   `src/app/core/http/api.interceptors.ts` (every refusal goes to the "no access" page, about
   line 170).

   **Fixed 9 November 2026** (console `3813c27`). The record no longer asks for a panel the reader's
   role does not open: home visits need `visit.view` and families `family.view`, the keys of their
   own pages, and the panel says "Your role does not open home visits, so none were read for this
   record." instead. A panel the server refuses says so in its place. The error interceptor no
   longer navigates on a refusal at all; only the route guards lead to "You do not have access to
   that section" (`DL-196`).

2. **Major. A barangay link's refused save throws the form away.** In ROS-05 step 3 the form offers
   every barangay, the API refuses "Dolores", and the website leaves for "You do not have access to
   that section", so step 4 cannot be done on the same form: fill it in again. Files: the same
   `api.interceptors.ts`, and `src/app/features/residents/resident-form-page.ts` (all five
   barangays offered, about line 123).

   **Fixed 9 November 2026** (console `3813c27`). The form offers a barangay link their own barangay
   only, already chosen. A refused save keeps the form and what was typed, and says above it "The
   save was refused, and nothing was saved.", the server's sentence, and "What you typed is still on
   the form.". ROS-05 is rewritten for it.

3. **Major. A barangay link's footer reads "Barangay Unknown barangay".** The website does not
   translate the API's barangay id for the signed-in account. Seen in ACC-02 and ACC-04. Files:
   `src/app/data/http/mappers/me.mapper.ts` (about line 85) and `src/app/layout/shell/shell.ts`
   (about line 83).

   **Fixed 9 November 2026** (console `3813c27`). The signed-in account's barangay is translated
   from the API's id to the console's through the same barangay list every other screen uses, so the
   footer reads "Barangay link · Barangay San Juan".

4. **Major. "Not found" is shown as a failure.** When the API answers that something does not
   exist, the page shows "Could not load this section" and one or more "Request failed" notices,
   never its own "not available" message. Seen in RPT-01; an unknown resident address shows three
   notices. Files: `src/app/core/http/api.interceptors.ts` (passes on its own error, about line
   199), while `src/app/data/http/api.client.ts` (about line 154) and
   `src/app/data/http/http-repositories.ts` (reports, about line 4287) look for a different one.

   **Fixed 9 November 2026** (console `3813c27`). One test, `isNotFound`, recognises the
   interceptor's failure, and every adapter uses it. A read that finds nothing raises no notice; the
   screen shows its own message ("That report is not available", "That resident is not available").
   Tested on both screens through the real interceptor.

5. **Major. Check-in mode does not say who was marked.** After "Attended", the panel is rebuilt
   and "Marked … as attended." is lost. Seen in EVT-18. Files:
   `src/app/features/events/event-detail-page.ts` (`onCheckedIn`, about line 243) and
   `src/app/features/events/event-check-in.ts` (about line 97).

   **Fixed 9 November 2026** (console `3813c27`). Marking somebody re-reads the counts, the list and
   the history, and no longer the event, so the panel stays and says "Marked … as attended.".

6. **Major. "My work" offers acts on a withheld task.** INTAKE is offered "Mark done" and "Move the
   date" on a protected case's task; the API refuses, and the website leaves for "You do not have
   access to that section". Seen in WRK-06. File: backend
   `src/modules/office-work/routes.ts` (every case task is marked manageable, about line 255).

   **Fixed 9 November 2026** (backend `dc1d571`, console `3813c27`). The API marks such a task not
   manageable for a reader it is withheld from. "My work" then offers no "Mark done" or "Move the
   date", and says "This task is on a case withheld from your account, so you cannot act on it. A
   colleague cleared for protected records can.". A refusal no longer leaves the page either. WRK-06
   expects this.

7. **Minor. The dashboard rounds pesos.** "Released by programme type" shows ₱3,001 for
   ₱3,000.50. File: `src/app/features/dashboard/dashboard-page.ts` (about line 47).

   **Fixed 9 November 2026** (console `3813c27`). The dashboard formats amounts with the one peso
   formatter: "₱3,000.50".

8. **Minor. The "Released" caption counts goods with money.** "₱3,000.50 Across 2 beneficiaries.
   Also 1 release of goods" when one person received the money. File:
   `src/app/features/dashboard/dashboard.copy.ts` (about line 65).

   **Fixed 9 November 2026** (console `3813c27`). With goods among the releases the caption reads
   "Money or goods to 2 beneficiaries. The amount is money only; 1 release of goods is counted, not
   valued.".

9. **Minor. The governance page says something untrue.** "Correction requests" ends "the previous
   value stays in the audit trail either way", but the trail keeps no values (DL-191). File:
   `src/app/domain/governance/correction-request.ts` (about line 228).

   **Fixed 9 November 2026** (console `3813c27`). It now ends "The audit trail records who changed
   the record and when, never what it said before or after.".

10. **Minor. A social worker's card says "Covers: All barangays".** A social worker sees assigned
    cases only. File: `src/app/features/administration/staff-page.html` (about line 62).

    **Fixed 9 November 2026** (console `3813c27`). "Covers" follows the office role: "Cases assigned
    to them" for a social worker, the barangay for a barangay link, "All barangays" for the others.
    GOV-01 reads it.

11. **Minor. Report sentences say "1 cases", "1 people", "1 households".** File: backend
    `src/modules/office-reports/series.ts` (about line 237).

    **Fixed 9 November 2026** (backend `dc1d571`). One of a thing is said in the singular: "1 case",
    "1 person served", "1 household".

12. **Minor. The registrant file gives times in UTC and codes.** "Starts" reads
    "2026-11-16T01:00:00.000Z" for a 09:00 event, and "Status" and "Attendance" read "registered"
    and "not-checked-in" rather than the words on screen. File: backend
    `src/modules/office-events/routes.ts` (about line 584).

    **Fixed 9 November 2026** (backend `dc1d571`). Times are Manila's with the zone stated
    ("2026-11-16 09:00 (Asia/Manila, UTC+08:00)"), the "Registered" column says its zone, and
    "Status" and "Attendance" read as on the screen. EVT-07 expects this.

Until staging is redeployed with these commits, ROS-05 and NFR-06 will fail there as they did
here, and a barangay link cannot read a resident's record at all.
