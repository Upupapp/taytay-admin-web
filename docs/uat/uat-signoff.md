# UAT sign-off: Taytay, Rizal LGU platform (staging)

Deliverable D6, "Testing and LGU UAT". Fill in one sheet per area once its cases have run, then the
defect summary, then the overall acceptance. The exit criteria are in [README.md](README.md).

Every name, signature and date below is left blank on purpose. Nobody signs for somebody else, and
nothing is signed before the cases it covers have run.

**Staging version tested** (filled in by the developer): console commit ................, API commit
................, app build .................

## How to fill in an area sheet

1. Count the area's cases by result: run, passed, failed and blocked.
2. List the ids of its open defects.
3. Tick one decision. "Accepted with open items" needs the open items listed.
4. The testers sign, then the MSWDO head accepts the area for the office.
