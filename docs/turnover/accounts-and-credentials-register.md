# Accounts and credentials register

Every account and secret the platform depends on: what it is for, who holds it now, where it is
kept, whether it passes to the LGU at turnover, and how to rotate or revoke it.

**This register holds no values.** No password, key, token or private file is written here, in
any repository, or in any other document of this package. Locations are described, not opened.
The credentials themselves are handed over one by one against this list, in person or through the
LGU's own secure channel (requirements D12 and NFR-SEC-6). Never send one by e-mail, chat, a
ticket, a screenshot or a shared document.

The three repositories are **public**. A secret committed to any of them must be treated as
exposed: rotate it at once, then remove it.

Where the repositories do not record who holds an account, the cell says "Not recorded" and is
left for the owner to fill in.

## Conflict to settle: who holds the Android keystore

`taytay-mobile-app/docs/release.md` says "Paul creates the keystore and keeps it".
`taytay-mobile-app/docs/integration/manual-tasks.md` §3 says the Android release keystore and the
Apple signing identity "must be generated and held by the municipality, not by a contractor".
The owner should decide which applies before the keystore is created: once the app is published,
every update must be signed with the same keystore, so whoever creates it holds the listing's
future.

## At turnover

For each row marked "Yes":

1. The current holder and the LGU's receiving officer meet, or use the LGU's own secure channel.
2. The LGU's officer receives the credential, signs in once to confirm it works, and changes the
   password or rotates the key where the provider allows.
3. The previous holder's access is removed, and both record the date in the README's "Handed
   over?" table.
