**How to read it.** Every requirement has an id, a priority, a check and a status, so it can be
approved, changed or struck one line at a time. Ids are never renumbered: a new requirement takes
the next number in its group.

Status:

- **Done** means the feature is built and its repository checks pass. It does not mean the check in
  "How we check it" has been run on staging. That is go-live item GL-10.
- **In progress** means it is being built now.
- **Planned** means it is the agreed direction but has not started, or it waits for a decision.
- **Proposed** means this version adds it because the law or good practice calls for it, but no
  source shows the project has decided it. Signing §9 adopts it, unless it is struck out.

Priority:

- **Must:** the platform cannot go live, or a deliverable cannot be accepted, without it. Every
  legal obligation is a Must.
- **Should:** important, but go-live could go ahead without it if the gap is written down.
- **Could:** wanted, or waiting for an owner's decision.
- What the platform will **not** do is listed as out of scope in §2.

The priorities are the preparer's proposal. They were drawn from the deliverables (§3), the launch
gate and the law (Appendix B), and signing §9 confirms them.

**How we check it** names the test, inspection or document that shows a requirement is met. A
person runs most checks on staging, with test accounts and test data. `check:…` names an automated
check in the console repository.

The requirements aim at the qualities ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148:2018 asks of a requirement: necessary,
unambiguous, singular, feasible and verifiable. Each one is traced to its source: a decision
(`DL-…`), a go-live item (`GL-…`), a launch-gate line, a law (Appendix B) or a TOR feature (`T-…`,
§3.2).

Most staff screens were built before staging went live. Their records say "not run against a live
API", so testing them on staging is still a go-live item (GL-10).

Paths are relative to each repository. `DL-…` ids point into the console's
`docs/reference-audit/decision-log.md`. Acronyms are explained in Appendix C.

## 2. Purpose and scope

The platform serves the residents of Taytay's five barangays and the staff of the Municipal Social
Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO). It has three parts:

| Part                                     | Who uses it                                                                                                                                               | What it does                                                                                                                                                           |
| ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Resident app "Taytay"** (Android, iOS) | Residents, in three states: guest, signed in but not yet verified, verified                                                                               | Creating an account, ID verification, the digital ID, the office directory, Balita news, Tulong information pages, messages to an office, and the resident's household |
| **Staff console** (web browser)          | MSWDO staff: intake officers, social workers, the MSWDO head, release (disbursing) officers, barangay focal persons, auditors and platform administrators | Reviewing IDs, running the app's content, the office inbox, and casework from intake to payout                                                                         |
| **API** (server)                         | Both apps. Nobody uses it directly                                                                                                                        | Stores all data and checks every permission. It is the only authority on what is allowed                                                                               |

**Out of scope for this plan** (the "won't do" items):

- Payments from residents to the LGU (taxes, permits): `DL-09`.
- Internal office forms: `DL-10`. `DL-10` also excluded residents' document requests. The TOR names
  Document Request (T-5, §3.2), so this version plans it (§4.14, phase 7), and a decision
  superseding that part of `DL-10` is written when phase 7 starts.
- Disaster response (Sakuna), which is in the mandate but deferred: `DL-11`.
- Accounting: no chart of accounts, ledger or bank link (`DL-89`). A release is tracked, not posted.
- SMS or e-mail notices to residents beyond sign-up codes. No gateway was supplied.
- Any tracking of where staff or residents are (`DL-86`).
- Staff features in the resident app, and resident features in the console. Each repository's
  `CLAUDE.md` forbids it.
- The citizen web portal and the verifier kiosk. They are named as channels in the mobile
  `CLAUDE.md`, but no repository exists for either.
- A Filipino-language interface, which is deferred but not rejected (`DL-18`).

### 9.1 Change control after approval

This procedure is Proposed and takes effect when §9 is signed.

1. The signed version is the baseline. Nothing in it changes without a new version.
2. A change is a new requirement, a change of wording or priority, or a struck line. It is asked for
   in writing, naming the id and the reason.
3. The developer states the change's effect on the plan, on other requirements and on Appendix B.
4. The owner decides. A change that touches §5.1 also goes to the DPO, and a change to an office
   convention also goes to the MSWDO head.
5. An approved change is issued as a new version and signed again in §9. A 1.x version covers
   wording, evidence and status. Version 2.0 is for adding, removing or substantially changing a
   Must requirement or a deliverable.
6. A status change backed by evidence, such as Planned to Done, is recorded in the version table
   without signing again.
7. Ids are never reused or renumbered. A struck requirement keeps its id, marked "Struck".
