# Module 10: Roster and Dashboard Orientation

Tracker item 10. Read [README.md](README.md) first for the room, the equipment and how the session
runs.

## 1. Purpose, who attends, time and what to bring

**Purpose.** After this session you can:

- register a resident in the office's registry, find them again, correct their record, and retire or
  restore it with a reason;
- record a household, a family and a relationship, and explain why a household is not a family;
- read a household's vulnerability snapshot as advice, and correct an indicator with a reason;
- explain what each role sees, and why a barangay link sees only its own barangay;
- read the dashboard, and open the list behind any figure;
- find your way to assistance requests, cases, releases and referrals.

**Who attends.** The MSWDO head, intake officers, social workers, release officers, barangay links
and the auditor. System administrators attend for the app accounts list and the residents' app
figures.

**Time.** About 3 hours, with a 10-minute break.

**What to bring.** Your staging test account. Nothing else: everything happens on the staff website.

**Words used in this module**

- **Registry** (or roster): the office's own list of the residents it serves, under "Residents". It
  is not the list of app accounts.
- **App account**: an account a resident created in the Taytay app. App accounts are listed under
  "Users and roles", which only the system administrator opens.
- **Household**: the people who live at one address.
- **Family**: people related to each other. A family is a claim about who belongs to whom.
- **Scope**: the part of the records your role may see: "All barangays", "Barangay San Juan" (one
  barangay) or "Assigned cases" (only what is assigned to you). Your scope is shown in the footer
  under the menu.
- **Retire**: take a record out of the active registry. Nothing is deleted.

## 3. Step-by-step guide

### 3.1 Register a resident

Who can: the system administrator, the MSWDO head, the intake officer, and a barangay link for their
own barangay.

1. Open "Residents" and click "Register a resident".
2. Fill "First name", "Last name", "Date of birth", "Sex" and "Civil status".
3. If you have the person's PhilSys card, type only the last four digits in "PhilSys — last four
   digits only". The full PhilSys number is never recorded here (RA 11055).
4. Fill "Barangay", "Street address" and "Mobile number". Fill "Monthly income (₱)" if known.
5. If the person belongs to a sector, tick it under "Sectors" (for example "Solo parent"), and say
   how you know in "How was this established?", for example _UAT: solo parent ID seen at the
   counter._
6. Click "Save record". If something is missing, the form lists it under "Check these before
   saving". When it is saved, the screen shows "Resident registered.".

A barangay link's form has no PhilSys or income field, and the server refuses a resident from
another barangay.

### 3.2 Find a resident

1. Open "Residents". Type a name in "Search".
2. Narrow with "Barangay" and "Sector". Click "Clear filters" to start again.
3. Retired records are hidden. Tick "Include retired records" to see them.

Addresses and numbers are not searched, as the hint under the filters says. Search by name.

### 3.3 Correct a record

1. Open the resident's record and click "Edit record".
2. Change only what is wrong and click "Save record". The screen shows "Resident record updated.".

### 3.4 Retire a record, and restore it

Who can: the MSWDO head and the system administrator.

1. Open the record and click "Retire record".
2. In the dialog "Retire this record?", fill "Reason". The "Retire record" button stays greyed out
   until you do.
3. Click "Retire record". The screen shows "Record retired.".
4. To bring it back, open the record (tick "Include retired records" to find it), click "Restore to
   active registry", give a reason and click "Restore record".

### 3.5 Register a household

1. Open "Households" and click "Register a household".
2. Under "Head of the household", type a name and choose the resident.
3. Choose "Barangay", fill "Street address", and fill "Monthly household income (pesos)". Tick
   "Classified indigent" only if the household has been classified so.
4. Click "Register household". The screen shows "Household registered.".

### 3.6 Add or remove a household member

1. Open the household and click "Edit members".
2. Under "Add someone to this household", search in "Find a resident" and choose the person. Set
   their role, for example "Child". They are tagged "Not saved yet".
3. To take someone out, click "Remove" beside them.
4. Fill "Why is this changing?". "Save changes" stays greyed out until you do.
5. Click "Save changes". The screen shows "Household members updated.".

"What has been done to this record" lists every change, with who made it, when, and the reason.
Earlier entries stay, even after a person leaves.

### 3.7 The vulnerability snapshot is advice

The household page shows a "Vulnerability snapshot". It says: "Advisory only. These indicators
describe what the records say about this household. They do not decide eligibility, entitlement or
any amount — a person does, and records why."

1. Click "Show the working" to see how each indicator was worked out and which records it read.
2. If an indicator is wrong, click "Correct this". Choose the right value in "What is actually the
   case?" and explain in "Why?". Click "Save correction". The indicator then reads "Corrected by a
   person".
3. To go back to the computed value, click "Use the computed value", then "Withdraw correction".

Who can correct: the social worker, the MSWDO head and the system administrator.

### 3.8 Record a family

**A household is not a family.** A household is an address. A family is a claim about who belongs to
whom. One household may hold several families, and a family may have no household while it is
between addresses.

1. Open "Families" and click "Record a family".
2. Fill "Name". Under "Members", use "Add a member" for each person. The first becomes "Family
   head"; set the others' roles, for example "Child".
3. Under "Where they live", find the household and click "Live here" (or leave it empty).
4. Fill "Why is this family being recorded?" and click "Record family". The screen shows "Family
   recorded.".

### 3.9 Record or end a relationship

Relationships are recorded person to person, so they survive either person moving.

1. On the family page click "Record a relationship".
2. Fill "This person", "is the" (for example "parent of"), "of", and "Why is this being recorded?".
   Click "Record relationship". The screen shows "Relationship recorded.".
3. To end one, use "End a relationship": "The relationship is kept and marked as ended. It is never
   deleted". Give "Why has it ended?" and click "Mark as ended".

### 3.10 Move a person out of a family

1. On the family page click "Move someone".
2. Choose the person in "Who is moving?" and the destination in "Which family are they joining?", or
   "No family — out of this one only".
3. Fill "Why?" and click "Record the move". The screen shows "The move has been recorded.", and the
   person is shown as "Former member". The earlier entries stay in the history.

### 3.11 Scope: who sees what

| Role                 | Residents, households, families                                               |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| System administrator | Every barangay. Registers residents. Opens "Users and roles".                 |
| MSWDO head           | Every barangay. Registers, retires and restores residents.                    |
| Intake officer       | Every barangay. Registers residents.                                          |
| Release officer      | Every barangay. Reads only.                                                   |
| Auditor              | Every barangay. Reads only; changes nothing.                                  |
| Barangay link        | Own barangay only. Registers residents there.                                 |
| Social worker        | No registry lists. Opens a person through a request or case assigned to them. |

Some details are withheld by role, and the screen says so with "Hidden by your role":

- PhilSys digits and monthly income are withheld from roles without clearance (for example the
  release officer and the barangay link).
- People in a protected sector (VAWC survivor, child in conflict with the law) appear as a surname
  and initial, such as "Halimbawa, C.", with the tag "Restricted", for roles without clearance.

Typing a page's address does not get past your role: you see "You do not have access to that
section".

### 3.12 The app accounts list (system administrator)

1. Open "Users and roles". It lists every account in the residents' app, residents and staff, with
   "Name", "Email", "Role" and "Office" only.
2. "Verification" narrows it to residents' accounts by where their ID verification stands: "Not
   verified", "Waiting for an ID review", "Verified" or "Not accepted". "Every account, residents
   and staff" shows them all.

This list is separate from the registry. A resident may have an app account and no registry record,
or the other way round. Changing roles is covered in module 12.

### 3.13 The dashboard

1. Open "Dashboard".
2. "What needs attention now" lists the work waiting for your role, such as "… requests waiting for
   approval". Each card says what it counted after "Counted:". Click "Review" to open the list.
3. The headline figures are "Open requests", "Awaiting approval", "Scheduled payouts" and
   "Released". "Released" is in pesos. Releases of goods are counted beside it, "counted and not
   valued", never given a peso value.
4. The tables are "Requests by status", "Requests by barangay" and "Released by programme type".
5. The foot of the page reads "Figures generated …" with the date and time. Click "Refresh" to count
   again.

**Every figure opens exactly the list it counted.** Click a figure or a table row and count the rows
in the list that opens: the number matches. For example:

- "Open requests" opens the request list with "Open requests only" ticked.
- A row of "Requests by barangay" opens that barangay's requests.
- "Scheduled payouts" opens the scheduled releases.
- "Released" opens the releases "Handed over (released, claimed or completed)" in the period.

**Filters.** Set "Barangay", "Programme type" or "Period" ("Any time", "Last 30 days", "Last 90
days"). The page says "Every figure below is calculated under this filter." Click "Clear filter" to
go back.

**By role.** Each role sees the figures of its own scope. Roles that do not open releases see
"Payout figures are not shown: your role does not open the releases they count." A social worker's
figures count only their assigned requests. A barangay link's count only their barangay.

**The residents' app (system administrator only).** "The residents’ app" shows "Resident accounts",
"ID reviews waiting" and "Digital IDs in force", and the table "Resident accounts by verification".
"ID reviews waiting" opens the ID review queue. "Digital IDs in force" opens "Digital IDs". Each row
of "Resident accounts by verification" opens "Users and roles" narrowed to that status.

**Quick actions.** "Record a request" opens a new assistance request. "Schedule payouts" opens the
releases waiting to be scheduled.

### 3.14 The rest of the console at a glance

These are covered in detail by the UAT pack
([../uat/uat-test-scripts.md](../uat/uat-test-scripts.md)). Every move in them asks for a reason and
is kept in the record's history.

- **"Assistance requests"** (REQ-01 to REQ-19). An intake is saved as a draft with no control
  number; filing it issues the number. The request moves through review, assessment, endorsement by
  a social worker and approval by the MSWDO head. The duplicate check advises; it never decides.
  Documents are recorded with the last four characters of their number only, and a file opens only
  after a warning.
- **"Cases"** (CAS-01 to CAS-12). A case is the office's continuing work with a person or household,
  and may hold several requests. The next step is an open task with an owner and a date. Only the
  MSWDO head closes a case, and a closed case stays closed: a new case names the old one.
- **"Releases"** (REL-01 to REL-10). A release hands over an approved amount or goods. Goods are
  described, never priced. A payout session counts released, deferred and unclaimed apart. The head
  approves but cannot release; the release officer releases but cannot approve.
- **"Referrals"** and **"Field visits"** (REF-01 to REF-12). A referral cannot be sent without a
  recorded lawful basis, and shares only the details chosen one at a time. Each visit observation
  says whose claim it is.

## 5. What the system deliberately does not do

- **It never decides eligibility, entitlement or an amount.** The vulnerability snapshot and the
  duplicate check are advice. A person decides, and records why.
- **It never deletes a resident, a member or a relationship.** It retires, removes with a reason, or
  marks as ended, and keeps the history.
- **It never records a full PhilSys number.** Only the last four digits.
- **It never treats a household as a family.**
- **It never searches the registry by address or number.** Search by name.
- **It never shows a figure you cannot check.** Every figure opens the list it counted, and says
  when it was counted.

## 6. Common questions and troubleshooting

**I cannot find a resident I registered.** Check the spelling and the filters, then click "Clear
filters". If the record was retired, tick "Include retired records".

**"Save record" shows "Check these before saving".** Read the list: each line names a field to fix.

**The "Retire record" or "Save changes" button is greyed out.** A reason is required. Type it first.

**I see "Hidden by your role".** Your role does not have clearance for that detail. This is by
design. Ask a colleague with clearance if the work needs it.

**As a social worker I have no "Residents" list.** By design: you open a person through a request or
case assigned to you.

**A figure and its list do not match.** Click "Refresh": the figures were counted at the time shown
in "Figures generated …". If they still differ, record it as a defect with both numbers.

**I see "You do not have access to that section".** Your role does not open that page. Click "Back
to dashboard".

## 7. Quick reference

**Residents**

- Register: "Residents" → "Register a resident" → "Save record".
- Find: "Search", "Barangay", "Sector"; "Include retired records".
- Correct: open the record → "Edit record".
- Retire or restore (head): "Retire record" / "Restore to active registry", with a reason.

**Households**

- "Households" → "Register a household" → "Register household".
- Members: "Edit members" → "Why is this changing?" → "Save changes".
- Snapshot: "Show the working"; "Correct this" → "Save correction". Advice only.

**Families**

- "Families" → "Record a family" → "Record family".
- "Record a relationship"; "End a relationship" → "Mark as ended".
- "Move someone" → "Record the move".

**Dashboard**

- Click any figure or row to open the list it counted.
- "Barangay", "Programme type", "Period"; "Clear filter"; "Refresh".
- "Figures generated …" says when it was counted.

**Remember**

- A household is an address; a family is who belongs to whom.
- Every change needs a reason, and the history keeps it.
- A barangay link sees its own barangay only. A social worker sees what is assigned to them.
